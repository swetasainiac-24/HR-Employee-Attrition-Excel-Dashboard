# HR Employee Attrition & Retention Analytics (Microsoft Excel)

## Project Overview
Employee turnover (attrition) directly impacts company productivity and hiring costs. In this HR Analytics project, I analyzed **1,470 employee records** across **36 workforce parameters** using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective was to uncover the root causes behind a **16.12% attrition rate** and build an interactive retention dashboard to help HR teams reduce staff turnover.

---

##  Tools & Skills Used
* **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
* **Key Techniques:** Pivot Tables, Dynamic Slicers, Statistical Correlation Analysis, KPI Tracking, Data Cleaning, and Interactive Dashboard Design.

---

## Workforce Summary Metrics
* **Total Employees Analyzed:** 1,470
* **Employees Who Left:** 237
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
* **Average Age of Employees Leaving:** ~33.6 Years
* **Average Monthly Income (Attrition Group):** $4,787
* **Average Tenure of Employees Leaving:** 5.13 Years (vs. 7.01 Years overall average)

---

## Key Insights & Findings
1. **Overtime is the #1 Burnout Driver:**
   * Employees working **Overtime** had a massive **30.53% attrition rate** (416 employees), compared to just **10.44%** for employees working standard hours.
2. **Department & Role Wise Turnover:**
   * **Sales Department** recorded the highest attrition at **20.63%**, followed by **Human Resources (19.05%)** and **Research & Development (13.84%)**.
   * Among specific job roles, **Laboratory Technicians (23.94%)** and **Sales Representatives** showed the highest turnover risk.
3. **Correlation Analysis with Attrition:**
   * **Total Working Years (-0.171)**, **Monthly Income (-0.160)**, and **Age (-0.159)** showed negative correlation with attrition—meaning younger, early-career employees with lower monthly income are much more likely to switch jobs.

##  HR Recommendations
* **Overtime & Workload Balancing:** Review workload distribution for teams with heavy overtime to bring down the 30.5% burnout-driven attrition.
* **Early-Career Retention Plan:** Introduce structured career progression and salary revisions for employees within their **first 2 to 5 years** at the company.
* **Targeted Sales & Lab Tech Support:** Revise incentive structures for Sales teams and improve job satisfaction programs for Laboratory Technicians.
